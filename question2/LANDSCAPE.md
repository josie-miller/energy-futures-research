# Emerging energy: who is building it, who pays, and what still has to work?

Initial landscape, checked 24 October 2026. Read alongside [cost and supply comparisons](ANSWER.md) and [technical evidence review](EVIDENCE_REVIEW.md).

**We are mapping the field now. The next stage will propose and test an ambitious ocean-focused system.** Funding does not prove performance, and a promising technology does not automatically become a cheap, financeable project.

The companies below are selected examples with relevant operating, project or financing evidence. This is not an exhaustive world ranking. “Leader” must mean something specific: metered output, repeated construction, manufacturing, independently verified performance, or access to capital. Those measures can point to different firms.

## How geopolitics changes deployment

Translate politics into a measurable effect rather than a vague country-risk score.

| Mechanism | Evidence | What to test in our later model |
|---|---|---|
| Concentrated manufacturing and trade restrictions | IEA's 2026 assessment reports China holds about 85% of solar and 80% of lithium-ion supply-chain production capacity, based on its referenced manufacturing data. Shares refer to capacity, not the ownership of every installation. [IEA](https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-technology-perspectives-2026/supply-chain-risks-and-industrial-competitiveness). | Regional equipment quotes, import delays, alternate suppliers, factory location and domestic-content conditions. The cheapest factory price may not be the delivered local price. |
| Nuclear fuel security | DOE's January 2026 awards allocate $900m each to American Centrifuge Operating and General Matter for HALEU capacity, and Orano Federal Services for LEU capacity, over a milestone-based program. [DOE](https://www.energy.gov/articles/us-department-energy-awards-27-billion-restore-american-uranium-enrichment). | Fuel availability by reactor design and year, contingency inventories, supplier concentration and schedule delays. Do not assume all small reactors need the same fuel. |
| Public support establishes early ocean markets | UK AR7 awarded 20.9 MW of tidal contracts across four projects; awarded capacity is not operating capacity. [UK government](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/new-auction-delivers-unprecedented-clean-homegrown-power). EU funding supports CorPower and Orbital projects above. | Supported versus unsubsidized economics, continuity of procurement, local manufacturing and the deployment volume needed for learning. |
| Strategic customers finance technology development | Computing buyers appear as investors, prospective power buyers and storage customers in several records above. | Separate equity from revenue contracts; inspect cancellation rights, delivery dates, creditworthiness and who bears overruns. A customer target cannot be added to an operating-demand ledger twice. |
| Ocean infrastructure crosses several jurisdictions | Permits, shipping, fisheries, cable routes, ports and data communications will depend on the selected site and architecture. This is a research mechanism, not a completed legal assessment. | Choose the actual location before assigning a permitting time or cost. Autonomous offshore activity does not mean no regulation. |

## Coverage still needed before we choose the mix

Extend this map to China, Korea, Japan and other deployment regions; mature solar/wind/storage manufacturers; existing and repeat-build nuclear suppliers; closed-loop and superhot geothermal; compressed-air, flow and thermal storage; electrolysers and fuels; tidal range, ocean thermal and salinity-gradient projects. In particular, compare more ocean firms and independent operating records rather than treating one company as the whole sector.

For each company, obtain current shareholder/control filings where available, the project owner's identity, capital required to finish, cost and schedule history, independent technical evidence and annual manufacturing capacity. Record unknowns explicitly. **This initial map narrows the research; it does not yet settle who will win or how much ocean energy our system should contain.**
